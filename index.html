About Me

Hello, stargazers! You may call me Soph, your guide through my little universe. Currently, I am a second-year and full-time student in the Bachelor of Arts in Multimedia Studies program at the University of the Philippines Open University.

If I were to describe myself in one word, it would be "dreamer." To others, it may be just a simple two-syllable word, but for me, its meaning goes far beyond what its length suggests. I firmly believe dreams are more than mere aspirations; they are the beginnings of the paths we dare to take, making life an adventure worth living.

Passion Projects

The Art of the Paws

The Art of the Paws is an Instagram-based small business my best friend and I established in 2021. Our primary goal is to share our artistic talents through commissioned pawtraits while advocating for animal welfare, as we donate a portion of our earnings to organizations that share our mission.

Visit our Instagram page!

Social Media Manager

The Summer Getaway Collection

Less than a year into the business, I secured a collaboration with Nini&Co, a local handmade pet bandana brand. My co-owner and I designed the entire collection in honor of my dog Oreo’s gotcha day. It sold out shortly after its launch and remains the brand’s biggest release to date.
